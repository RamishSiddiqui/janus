# Glossary

**Adapter**
The kind of service a provider connects to (Ollama, OpenRouter, ComfyUI, and so on).

**Branch**
A new conversation started from a message in another one, keeping the story up to that point.

**Canon memory**
A memory promoted to a character-level fact that holds across all of that character's chats.

**Cast**
The characters in a conversation, including detected NPCs.

**Character card**
A character's definition in the standard V2 format, often embedded in a PNG image.

**Context window**
The amount of text, measured in tokens, a model can see at once.

**Embedding**
A list of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text, used to find related passages.

**Emotional state**
A character's current mood, trust, intensity and dominant emotion, tracked after each reply.

**Lorebook**
Short world-building entries added to the prompt only when their keywords appear (or always, if marked so).

**Memory**
A lasting fact about the story, pinned by you or extracted automatically.

**Memory scope**
Whether a chat shares memories with the character's other chats, keeps its own, or uses none.

**Model**
A specific AI a provider offers. You enable the ones you want to use.

**NPC**
A character the story introduced, detected automatically and added to the cast.

**Persona**
You, as a character in the story.

**Post-history instructions (Narrative Direction)**
Instructions placed after the conversation in the prompt to shape how a reply ends.

**Preset (image)**
A saved bundle of image-generation settings.

**Provider**
A service or program that supplies models: chat, image, video, voice or embeddings.

**RAG**
Retrieval-augmented generation: finding relevant past messages and memories and adding them to the prompt.

**Recovery key**
A one-time key created with an account, used to set a new passphrase if it is forgotten.

**Rolling summary**
A short summary of older messages that no longer fit, kept in the prompt as "the story so far".

**Scene state**
The current location, who is present, ambience and mood of the story.

**Timeline**
One of several alternative replies to the same message.

**Token**
A piece of text (a short word or part of a word) that models count when measuring length.

**Trash**
Where deleted conversations, characters and personas wait before being removed for good.
